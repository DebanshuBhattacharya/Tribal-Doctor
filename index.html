OUR PURPOSE

Wisdom passed down. A legacy worth preserving.

For generations, indigenous communities have preserved valuable knowledge of medicinal plants, forests and traditional wellness practices. Yet much of this wisdom remains undocumented, difficult to discover and at risk of being lost.

Tribal Doctor connects this knowledge with technology, creating opportunities to discover, preserve and share botanical heritage while recognising the communities behind it.

Knowledge lives beyond books.

Generations of botanical knowledge exist within communities, passed down through observation, experience and practice. Tribal Doctor aims to document, preserve and connect this knowledge with the wider world.

The forest is part of the product.

Responsible sourcing begins with protecting the ecosystems that make these plants possible.

  • Sustainable harvesting

    Collection methods designed to let plants and habitats regenerate.

  • Seasonal harvesting

    Timing collection to natural growth cycles rather than constant demand.

  • Biodiversity

    Awareness of the wider ecosystem each plant belongs to, not just the yield.

  • Traceability

    A visible record connecting every product back to where and how it was sourced.
