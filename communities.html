How Tribal Doctor helps communities

Four commitments shape everything we build.

  1. Source stays attached
  2. Credit in your words
  3. Consent you can withdraw
  4. A way in for the young

Knowledge stays with its source

Each record keeps the plant, the place and the teacher together. A name is never separated from the valley it comes from.

Credit, in the keeper's words

Contributors choose how they are named, or not named at all. The record carries that choice wherever it goes.

Consent that can be withdrawn

Nothing is published without agreement. A contributor can ask for a record to be changed or removed.

A way in for the next generation

Identify a plant from a photo, search local names and read traditional use in one place, so young people can find what their elders know.
